Quick answer

PG Soft, short for Pocket Games Soft, is a mobile-first slot studio founded in 2015 and based in Malta. In the Philippines it is known for Piñata Wins, Wild Bounty Showdown, Wild Ape and Lucky Neko: portrait-friendly games with cascades, gold-framed symbols and growing multipliers. About PG Soft

According to its company page, PG Soft was founded in 2015, has its registered address in Valletta, Malta, and designs games for phones first. It says its products are tested and certified by BMM and Gaming Associates, and it shows Malta (MGA) and Gibraltar licensing on its site.

PG Soft publishes RTP, volatility and max win on each official game page, so those figures on DemoLang come straight from the provider. Feature details such as free-spin counts come from slot databases and are marked as such.
