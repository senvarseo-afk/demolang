About JILI

JILI's official site lists online slots, bingo, table and card games, and fishing games. Its best-known titles here are Super Ace, the Fortune Gems series and Money Coming. JILI's site does not state a founding year or head office; trade press (AGBrief, January 2026) reports it was founded in 2019 and that its releases are certified by testing labs such as GLI and BMM.

JILI's own game pages publish volatility, max win and layout but not RTP. The RTP figures on DemoLang's JILI pages therefore come from slot databases and are marked third-party.

Sources: JILI company page, AGBrief
