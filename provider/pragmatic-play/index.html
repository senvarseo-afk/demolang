About Pragmatic Play

Pragmatic Play's About page names Gibraltar as its headquarters and lists slots, live casino, crash and arcade games. It says it is licensed or certified in more than 30 jurisdictions, including the UK Gambling Commission and Gibraltar, and that its random number generators are tested by GLI, Quinel and Gaming Associates. Trade press dates the company to 2015.
