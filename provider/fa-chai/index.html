About Fa Chai

Fa Chai's About page says the company was established in 2019, describes itself as rooted in Asia and claims more than 70 games across slots, fishing, arcade and table games. It is operated by WiseInNet Technologies B.V. under a Curaçao Gaming Authority licence and says its games are certified by GLI.

Fa Chai's game pages list RTP, volatility, max win, ways and release date. Feature details such as free-spin counts and buy prices are not on those pages; DemoLang takes them from SlotCatalog and marks them.
