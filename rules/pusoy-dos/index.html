CARD GAME RULES

Pusoy Dos Rules at Card Ranking

By DemoLang Editorial Team · Updated 2026-11-11

Short answer

Pusoy Dos is the Filipino version of Big Two. Four players get 13 cards each, and the goal is to be the first to empty your hand. Whoever holds the 3 of clubs leads first. On your turn, beat the last play with the same number of cards (single, pair, triple or 5-card hand) or pass. 2 is the highest card, and in the usual Philippine order diamonds is the highest suit.

Card ranking

From highest to lowest: 2, A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3. Ang 2 ang pinakamataas, kaya "Dos" ang pangalan.

Suit order (Philippines)

SuitRank
Diamonds ♦1 (highest)
Hearts ♥2
Spades ♠3
Clubs ♣4 (lowest)

Suit only matters when two cards have the same rank: 7♦ beats 7♥. Ibang bansa, ibang order: standard Big Two in Hong Kong ranks spades highest. Pag naglaro kayo, pag-usapan muna ang house rules.

5-card hands, lowest to highest

HandWhat it isHow ties are broken
Straight5 cards in sequence, e.g. 5-6-7-8-9Lowest 5-card hand. Compare the top card, then its suit. A can be low (A-2-3-4-5) or high (10-J-Q-K-A); J-Q-K-A-2 is not allowed.
Flush5 cards of one suitCompare suit first, then the top card.
Full house3 of a kind + a pairCompare the 3 of a kind.
Four of a kind + 14 of a kind + any cardCompare the 4 of a kind. Four cards alone cannot be played.
Straight flushStraight, all one suitHighest hand.

A higher 5-card hand beats any lower one, e.g. any flush beats any straight.

Paano laruin, step by step

  1. Deal. Shuffle a 52-card deck and deal 13 cards to each of 4 players.
  2. First lead. The player with the 3♣ starts. In later games, the winner of the last game leads.
  3. Lead a combination. Play a single, a pair, a triple or a 5-card hand. Triples cannot carry an extra card.
  4. Follow or pass. Going around, each player either plays a higher combination with the same number of cards, or passes. Passing is allowed even if you can play, and you may play again later in the same round.
  5. Round ends. When everyone else passes in a row, the last player who played wins the round and leads anything they like.
  6. Winning. The first player to play their last card wins. In common Philippine scoring the winner gets 1 point, doubled for each 2 in the final play (2, 4, 8 or 16 points). Some groups keep playing to rank everyone: 5, 3, 1 and 0 points.

2 or 3 players

With 3 players, deal 17 cards each; the last card goes to whoever holds the lowest 3. With 2 players, groups deal 13, 17 or 21 cards each. These splits come from Big Two in general, so agree on them before you start.

Pusoy Dos vs Pusoy

Hindi sila pareho. Pusoy is the Filipino form of Chinese Poker: you arrange your 13 cards into three poker hands and compare them. Pusoy Dos is a race to get rid of your cards.
