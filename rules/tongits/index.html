Tongits Rules: Paano Laruin ang Tong-its

By DemoLang Editorial Team · Updated 2026-11-12

Short answer

Tongits (Tong-its) is a Filipino rummy game for three players with a 52-card deck. The dealer gets 13 cards and the others get 12. On each turn you draw, lay down sets or runs, add to melds on the table (sapaw), then discard. You win by using up all your cards (Tong-its), by calling Draw with the lowest points, or by having the lowest points when the stock runs out. A player who never lays down a meld is burned (sunog) and loses.

Players, baraha at deal

  • 3 players, one standard 52-card deck, no jokers.
  • The dealer gets 13 cards, the other two get 12 each. The 15 cards left are the stock, face down.
  • The first dealer is chosen at random; after that, the winner of the last round deals.
  • The dealer starts by laying down any melds and discarding, without drawing first.

Halaga ng baraha

BarahaPoints
A (Ace)1
2 hanggang 10Face value
J, Q, K10

Points only matter at the end: you count the cards still in your hand that are not part of a meld. Lower is better.

Melds (bahay)

  • Set: 3 or 4 cards of the same rank, e.g. 7♠ 7♥ 7♦.
  • Run: 3 or more cards in a row of the same suit, e.g. 4♣ 5♣ 6♣. Ace is low only: A-2-3 works, Q-K-A does not.
  • A card can belong to only one meld at a time.

Bawat turn, step by step

  1. Draw. Take the top card of the stock, or the top card of the discard pile. You may take the discard only if it makes a new meld with at least two cards from your hand, and you must lay that meld down right away.
  2. Expose melds. Optionally lay down melds from your hand. Laying down at least one meld "opens" you. A set of four can be laid face down so it still counts as a secret set.
  3. Sapaw (lay off). Optionally add cards from your hand to any meld on the table, yours or an opponent's. There is no limit per turn.
  4. Discard. End your turn by discarding one card face up.

Paano natatapos ang round

1. Tong-its

If every card in your hand can be laid down as melds or laid off on the table, call Tong-its. You win the round at once.

2. Draw (o "fight")

At the start of your turn, before drawing, you may call Draw if you have opened and think your points are the lowest. You cannot call it if someone did sapaw on your melds since your last turn, or if you did sapaw on your own melds on your previous turn.

Each other player then folds or challenges. Only a player who has opened may challenge. If both fold, the caller wins. If anyone challenges, the caller and the challengers show their hands and the lowest points win. A challenger beats the caller on a tie.

3. Ubos ang stock

When the last stock card has been drawn and that turn is finished, the round ends. Players who never opened lose automatically. The opened player with the lowest points wins. On a tie, the player who took the last card wins if they are in the tie; otherwise the tied player sitting to the right of the last-card taker wins.

Sunog (burned)

If the round ends and you have not laid down a single meld, you are burned. You lose, cannot challenge a Draw, and pay an extra penalty to the winner. Sapaw alone does not open you.

Puntos at chips

Groups usually play for chips. One common count, from pagat.com:

SituationWinner gets
Panalo (any win)1 chip from each loser
Panalo by Tong-its3 chips from each loser, instead of 1
Panalo ng Draw after a challenge3 chips from each loser
Each Ace in the winner's hand or own melds+1 chip from each loser
Each secret set of four the winner has+3 chips from each loser
Loser is burned (sunog)+1 chip from that loser

Many groups also keep a side pot that goes to whoever wins two rounds in a row. Amounts vary a lot by group, so agree before the first deal.

Mga house rule na nag-iiba

  • Q-K-A run: some groups allow it; the classic rules do not.
  • Tie on a Draw: classic rules give it to the challenger; some groups give it to the caller.
  • Taking the discard: classic rules only allow it to make a new meld with two cards from your hand; some casual games are looser.
  • Extra bonuses: for example a run of five or more, or 2 chips instead of 3 for the big wins.

Tongits vs Pusoy Dos vs Lucky 9

Tongits is a rummy game: you build sets and runs and try to finish with the fewest points. Pusoy Dos is a race to empty your hand by beating the last play. Lucky 9 is a quick banker game where the hand closest to 9 wins.
