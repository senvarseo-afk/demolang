Lucky 9 Rules: Paano Laruin

[Author name] · Updated 2026-11-12

Short answer

Lucky 9 is a Filipino card game where each player tries to get a hand closest to 9. Everyone plays against the banker (bangka). Each hand starts with two cards: 2 to 9 count at face value, aces are 1, and 10, J, Q and K are 0. Only the last digit of the total counts, so 7 + 8 = 15 is worth 5. Two cards totalling 9 is a Lucky 9 and usually wins at once.

Paano bilangin ang kamay

Add up the cards and keep only the last digit. The highest possible hand is 9 and the lowest is 0.

Paano laruin, step by step

  1. Pumili ng bangka. One player or the dealer acts as banker. Every other player plays only against the banker, not against each other. Up to six players is common.
  2. Deal. Each player and the banker get two cards.
  3. Lucky 9. If your first two cards total 9, show them right away. You win unless the banker also has a two-card 9.
  4. Pangatlong baraha. Each player may ask for one more card or stay. The third card is the last one; you cannot draw a fourth.
  5. Bangka draws. The banker then decides on a third card. Many tables use a fixed rule, for example drawing on 4 or less and staying on 5 or more, which is the rule in one casino version of the game.
  6. Compare. Each player's total is compared with the banker's. Closer to 9 wins.

Mga house rule na nag-iiba

Sources and tables disagree on a few points, so agree on them before you start:

  • Third card for the banker: free choice in some home games, a fixed "draw on 4 or less" rule in others.
  • Tabla (tie): guides commonly treat it as a push, where nobody wins the round. Check your table's rule.
  • Who becomes banker next: decided by the group before playing.

Lucky 9 vs Baccarat vs Pusoy Dos

Lucky 9 is counted the same way as Baccarat (last digit, face cards 0), which is why it is sometimes called the Filipino Baccarat. The difference is that in Lucky 9 each player decides on their own third card. Pusoy Dos is a different kind of game: a race to empty your hand, with 2 as the highest card.

May diskarte ba?

The only real choice is whether to take a third card. With a low total (0 to 4) a third card can only help or leave you low; with 7 or 8 a third card is more likely to make the hand worse. Beyond that, the cards are random: there is no pattern that makes a Lucky 9 "due". DemoLang does not give tips for playing for money.
