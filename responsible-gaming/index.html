Responsible gaming

Demos are for fun and for learning how games work. They are not practice for winning money.

21 pataas lamang

DemoLang is for adults 21 and over. In the Philippines, persons under 21 are prohibited from gaming establishments and online gaming platforms (PAGCOR, under PD 1869 as amended by RA 9487).

Demo lang, hindi training

A demo cannot teach you to win. Slot results are random, so a good run in a demo means nothing for real money. Use demos to see how a game works and how rarely its big features come.

Mga senyales na kailangan mo ng tulong

  • You play to win back money you lost.
  • You borrow, sell things or skip bills to keep playing.
  • You hide how much time or money you spend.
  • You feel restless or irritable when you try to stop.
  • Gambling is hurting your work, studies or family.

Saan hihingi ng tulong (Philippines)

  • National Problem Gambling Helpline (PAGCOR and Seagulls Flock Organization, 24/7): (02) 8248-9568, Viber +63 998 207 2621, npgh.ph.
  • Self-exclusion: you can bar yourself from PAGCOR-licensed gaming through the Online Self-Exclusion Application, osea.pagcor.ph.
  • NCMH Crisis Hotline (24/7, for emotional crisis): 1553 from a landline, 1800-1888-1553, 0919-057-1553 (Smart/TNT), 0917-899-8727 (Globe/TM).
  • Hopeline Philippines (24/7): 0917-558-4673, 0918-873-4673, (02) 8804-4673.
  • Emergency: 911.
