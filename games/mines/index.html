Paano laruin ang Mines

  1. Pumili ng taya at kung ilang mines (1 hanggang 24) ang itatago sa 25 na tile.
  2. I-tap ang Start. Nakatago na ang mines bago ka pumili; hindi na sila gumagalaw.
  3. Magbukas ng tile. Kung diamond, tataas ang multiplier. Kung mine, talo ang taya.
  4. I-tap ang Cash out anumang oras para kunin ang taya × multiplier.

Mas maraming mines, mas mataas ang multiplier, pero mas maliit ang tsansang ligtas ang bawat tile.

May diskarte ba?

Short answer

Wala. Tsansa × multiplier ay laging 0.97, kaya kahit ilang mines o kailan ka mag-cash out, 97% ang average na balik. Walang tile na mas ligtas sa iba: random ang puwesto ng mines sa bawat round.

"97% win rate" na strategy?

Videos that promise a 90%+ win rate usually pick 1 mine and cash out after one tile. You do win 96% of rounds that way, but each win pays only 1.01x, and the 4% of losses take the whole bet. Over many rounds you still end at about 97% of what you bet. A high win rate is not the same as making money.

Mines predictor

Apps and Telegram bots that claim to show where the mines are cannot see the game's hidden board. In this game the mines are placed with your browser's secure random generator the moment you tap Start, and nothing on the page can know them before you pick.
