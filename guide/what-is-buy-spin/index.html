What Is Buy Spin?

By DemoLang Editorial Team · Updated 2026-11-13

Short answer

Buy spin, also called bonus buy or feature buy, lets you pay a fixed price, usually 50x to 100x your bet, to start a slot's free spins right away instead of waiting for scatters. The bonus you buy plays the same way as one you trigger, but it can still pay back less than you paid for it. It costs a lot per round and is switched off in some countries.

Paano gumagana

  1. Set your bet, for example ₱10.
  2. Tap the Buy or Buy Feature button. The game shows the price, for example 100x = ₱1,000.
  3. The game starts the free spins round straight away, with the usual scatter count already on the reels.
  4. Whatever the bonus pays is your result. It may be more or less than the ₱1,000 you paid.

Mas sulit ba ang buy spin?

  • Hindi ito shortcut sa panalo. The price is set so that, on average, the bought bonus pays back about the same share as the rest of the game. Some games publish a separate RTP for the buy, close to the normal one.
  • Mabilis ang galaw ng pera. One buy at 100x equals 100 normal spins in a single tap, and many bonuses pay less than their price.
  • Bawal sa ilang bansa. Bonus buy is not offered in some markets, such as the UK, which is why some game versions do not show the button.

Subukan nang libre

In a demo, buying the bonus costs only virtual credits. Buy it ten times in Sweet Bonanza 1000 and write down what each bonus paid compared with the 100x price. You will see the spread for yourself. Related: demo vs real money.
