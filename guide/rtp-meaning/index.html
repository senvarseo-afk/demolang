RTP Meaning: Ano ang Return to Player?

By DemoLang Editorial Team · Updated 2026-11-13

Short answer

RTP (return to player) is the share of all money bet on a slot that the game is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. A 96% RTP means that across millions of spins by all players, about 96 of every 100 pesos bet come back as wins and 4 stay with the operator. It is a long-run average, not a promise for your session, and it does not tell you anything about the next spin.

Paano basahin ang RTP

RTPSa bawat ₱100 na taya, sa katagalanHouse edge
97%₱97 bumabalik3%
96.5%₱96.50 bumabalik3.5%
94.5%₱94.50 bumabalik5.5%
92.1%₱92.10 bumabalik (perya Color Game)7.9%

The "house edge" is simply 100% minus the RTP: the part the game keeps on average.

Ang hindi sinasabi ng RTP

  • Hindi ito para sa isang session. In 200 spins you can end far above or far below 96%. The figure only settles down over a huge number of spins. The UK Gambling Commission makes the same point: actual results move closer to the designed RTP as more play happens.
  • Hindi ito "tangke" na napupuno. A game that has not paid for a while does not owe anyone. Each spin is random and separate. See scatter meter: legit ba?
  • Hindi nito sinasabi kung gaano kadalas ang panalo. Two games with the same RTP can feel completely different. That is volatility.

Bakit may iba't ibang RTP ang isang laro

Many providers release the same game in several RTP versions and the operator picks one. Examples from games we checked:

GameRTP versions
Sweet Bonanza 100096.53%, 95.52%, 94.51%
Gates of Olympus 100096.50%, 95.51%, 94.50%
Sugar Rush 100096.00% on Pragmatic's page; 95.50% to 97.50% in other listings
Pinata Wins96.76% (one version listed by PG Soft)

So "Sweet Bonanza 1000 has 96.53%" is only true for the top version. Some games also have a separate RTP for the bonus buy. Our game pages show every version we found, with the source and date checked.

Mataas na RTP = mas madaling manalo?

Only a little, and only over a very long time. The difference between 96.5% and 94.5% is 2 pesos per 100 bet on average. In a normal session, luck and volatility matter far more. A higher RTP is still the better deal if you play for money, which is why it is worth knowing.
