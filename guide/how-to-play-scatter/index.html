How to Play Scatter: Paano Maglaro ng Slot

By DemoLang Editorial Team · Updated 2026-11-13

Short answer

To play a scatter slot: set your bet, tap Spin, and the reels stop on random symbols. You win when matching symbols line up the way that game pays (on lines, on adjacent reels, or anywhere on the grid). Landing enough scatter symbols, usually three or four, starts free spins, where multipliers often make wins bigger. You can learn all of this for free in a demo before ever risking money.

Step by step

  1. Pumili ng laro. Start with a low-volatility game like Super Ace or Fortune Gems; wins come more often, so it is easier to see how things work.
  2. I-set ang taya. Use the + and − buttons. In a demo the credits are virtual, so any amount is fine.
  3. I-tap ang Spin. Each spin is random and separate from the last one.
  4. Tingnan ang panalo. Winning symbols light up and the amount is added to your balance. In many games they then disappear and new symbols fall in (a cascade or tumble), which can win again.
  5. Hintayin ang scatters. Enough scatter symbols anywhere on the screen start free spins. What counts as "enough" depends on the game.
  6. Buksan ang paytable. The ⓘ or menu button shows every symbol's value and the feature rules. This is the most useful screen in any slot.

Ang scatter at free spins

A scatter pays or triggers a feature wherever it lands; it does not need a line. Some examples:

  • Super Ace: 3 scatters give 10 free spins with a bigger combo multiplier.
  • Pinata Wins: 3 Star Piñata scatters give 15 free spins.
  • Sweet Bonanza: 4 or more scatters give 10 free spins with multiplier bombs.
