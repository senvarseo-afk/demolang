Volatility Meaning sa Slots

By DemoLang Editorial Team · Updated 2026-11-13

Short answer

Volatility (also called variance) describes how a slot pays, not how much. A low-volatility slot pays small wins often; a high-volatility slot can go a long time with little or nothing, then pay a big win. Two games with the same RTP can have very different volatility. Providers label it themselves, usually as low, medium or high.

Volatility vs RTP

RTP tells you how much a game pays back on average over millions of spins. Volatility tells you how bumpy the ride is on the way there. Think of two jeepneys with the same fare: one stops at every corner, the other goes fast but only stops at the terminal. Same price, very different trip.

Each provider uses its own scale. JILI writes labels like "LOW-MED" and "HIGH-MED"; PG Soft uses low, medium and high. There is no shared formula behind the labels, so compare games from the same provider rather than across providers.

Bakit mahalaga ito

  • Mahabang tuyo ay normal sa high volatility. It is not a sign the game is "cold" or broken. See scatter time pattern for why waiting for the right hour does not help.
  • Mas mabilis maubos ang budget on high-volatility games, because most spins pay little.
  • Subukan muna sa demo. Play 100 spins of a low and a high game with virtual credits and compare how your balance moves. That shows volatility better than any table.
