Editorial policy

Every game page is written and checked by a person before it is indexed.

Saan galing ang game specs

RTP, volatility, max win, reel layout and release date come from the provider's own game page or game sheet first. When the provider does not publish a value, we use a well-known slot database and name it as the source. Each spec table shows the date it was checked and links to its sources.

Some games come in more than one RTP version. When the provider lists several, we show all of them, because the version you meet elsewhere may not be the highest one.

Paano isinusulat ang pages

AI tools help with research and first drafts. Before a page is opened to Google, a person plays the demo, checks every number and feature against the source and the in-game paytable, rewrites the copy and is responsible for what is published. Pages that have not passed this check stay out of Google's index.

Ang hindi namin ginagawa

  • No "sure win", "scatter time" or pattern claims. Slot results are random.
  • No links to casinos, betting apps or bonus offers, and no paid placements.

Corrections

Found a wrong number? Tell us on the contact page with the game name and your source. We check it against the provider, fix the page and update the checked date.
