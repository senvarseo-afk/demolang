Quick answer

Sweet Bonanza 1000 is Pragmatic Play's 2024 remake of Sweet Bonanza with bigger multipliers. It pays anywhere on a 6x5 grid when 8 or more matching symbols land, tumbles after every win, and in free spins drops candy bombs worth x2 to x1,000. Pragmatic lists a 96.53% RTP and 25,000x max win; volatility is high. Try the Sweet Bonanza 1000 demo free here.

Paano laruin

Pay anywhere and tumble

There are no lines. Eight or more of the same symbol anywhere on the 6x5 grid is a win. Winning symbols disappear, new ones fall, and it continues while new wins form.

Free spins and candy bombs

Four or more lollipop scatters give 10 free spins (3 or more during the bonus add 5). Only in free spins, candy bomb multipliers from x2 to x1,000 can land. They stay through the tumble sequence, are added together and applied to the total win of that spin.

Ante bet and buy

Ante bet costs 25% more and doubles the free-spin chance. Where allowed, free spins can be bought for 100x, or a 500x Super Bonus where every bomb is at least x20.

Taglish tip: Kung ikukumpara mo sa original, mas madalang pero mas malaki ang bombs dito. Subukan ang dalawang demo nang magkasunod para makita mismo.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
Ano ang pinagkaiba ng Sweet Bonanza 1000 sa original?
The free-spin bombs go up to x1,000 instead of x100, the max win rises from about 21,100x to 25,000x, and volatility is higher.
Ano ang ante bet?
Ante bet raises the stake by 25% and roughly doubles the chance of triggering free spins. It is not offered everywhere.
