Paano laruin

Golden symbols at multiplier frames

Golden symbols land on reels 2 to 5. When one is part of a win, DJ Penguin turns it into a Wild inside a gold frame with a multiplier. Each new hit on that frame raises its multiplier, so the frames are where the bigger wins come from.

Free game

Three scatters give 10 free spins. The free game starts with multiplier frames already placed: three scatters give three frames, up to six frames for six scatters. Collecting scatters during the bonus adds frames, and 3 or more scatters add 5 spins, up to 50 spins in total. The built-up multipliers reset when the bonus ends.

Six reels, 3,600 ways

The middle reels are taller (5 rows) than the outer ones (3 rows). Wins pay left to right on adjacent reels, in any row position.

Taglish tip: Medium-high ang volatility nito, kaya normal na maraming tuyong spin bago pumasok ang free game. Sa demo mo makikita kung gaano kadalas talaga ito.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
