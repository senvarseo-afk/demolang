Quick answer

Money Coming is a JILI number slot with no paylines to learn: the digits that land across three reels simply form your prize. A fourth special reel can add a multiplier, a respin or the Lucky Wheel. JILI rates it low-to-medium volatility with a 10,000x max win, and SlotCatalog lists a 97% RTP. It is one of the simplest JILI games to understand. Try the Money Coming demo free here.

Paano laruin

Three number reels

Instead of symbols on lines, the three main reels show digits (and blanks). Read them left to right and that is your prize. A blank in the wrong place can turn a big number small, which is the whole tension of the game.

The special fourth reel

The fourth reel only counts when the main reels already show a win. It can land a multiplier (x2, x5 or x10), a Respin, or a scatter that starts the Lucky Wheel. SlotCatalog notes that some of these options only appear above certain bet levels.

Lucky Wheel

The wheel always lands on a prize, and this is where the 10,000x top win comes from.

FAQ

Demo vs real money: ano ang pinagkaiba?
The demo uses virtual credits and pays nothing real. Note that some special-reel options only unlock at higher bet levels, so what you see depends on the bet you set in the demo.
What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
Paano manalo sa Money Coming?
The numbers on the three main reels read as one amount, and that is the win. The special fourth reel only pays when the main reels already make a win.
May free spins ba ang Money Coming?
No. There are no free spins, wilds or buy feature. The bonus is the Lucky Wheel, triggered from the special reel, which always gives a prize.
