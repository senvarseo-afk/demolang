Quick answer

Super Ace is JILI's card-themed slot and one of the most played games in the Philippines. It runs on a 5x4 grid with 1,024 ways, low-to-medium volatility and a third-party listed RTP of 97%. Wins cascade, a combo multiplier climbs to x5 (x10 in free spins), and golden cards flip into Joker wilds. JILI lists a 10,000x max win. Play the Super Ace demo here free, with virtual credits only.

Paano laruin

Cascade at combo multiplier

Every winning card is removed and new cards drop in. Each cascade in the same spin raises the combo multiplier: x1, x2, x3, x5. It resets at the next spin. This is why a small first win can turn into a bigger one.

Golden cards at Jokers

Golden-framed cards appear on reels 2 to 4. When a golden card is part of a win, it turns into a Joker wild. A Big Joker also turns 1 to 4 more cards into wilds; a Little Joker stays a single wild.

Scatter at free spins

Three scatters give 10 free spins. Three more during the bonus add 5 spins, with no limit. In free spins the combo multiplier doubles: x2, x4, x6, x10.

Taglish tip: Sa Super Ace, ang laki ng panalo ay galing sa sunod-sunod na cascade, hindi sa isang spin lang. Sa demo, bilangin kung ilang beses ka talagang umabot sa x5. Doon mo makikita kung bakit hindi ito “pattern” na mahuhulaan.

The demo is JILI's own build with virtual credits; you cannot cash out anything. JILI does not publish an RTP on its game page, so the 97% figure comes from SlotCatalog and Bigwinboard, and a casino may run a different version. Use the demo to learn how golden cards and the combo multiplier behave.
What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
Ano ang golden card sa Super Ace?
Golden cards land on reels 2 to 4. When one is part of a win, it flips into a Joker wild instead of disappearing. A Big Joker also turns 1 to 4 other cards on reels 2 to 5 into wilds; a Little Joker turns only its own spot wild.
May buy feature ba ang Super Ace?
No. None of the sources we checked list a buy feature or extra bet for the original Super Ace. Free spins only come from three scatters.
