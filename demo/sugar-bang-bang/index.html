Quick answer

Sugar Bang Bang is a candy-themed slot from Fa Chai, released in August 2022. It plays on five reels with 1,024 ways to win (2,000 with Extra Bet), has a listed RTP of 96.5% with medium volatility, and pays up to 3,000x the bet. Its signature is cascading wins with a multiplier that climbs to x5 in the base game and x10 in free spins. You can try it here free, with virtual credits only.

Paano laruin

Cascades at elimination multiplier

Every win removes the paying symbols and new candy drops into the gaps, so one spin can pay several times. Each cascade in the same spin pushes the multiplier up: x1, x2, x3, then x5. It resets on the next spin.

Golden symbols at Wild

Golden symbols can appear on reels 2 to 4. When a golden symbol is part of a win and gets eliminated, it turns into a Wild instead of disappearing, which keeps the cascade going.

Scatter at free spins

Three gummy-bear scatters, including ones that drop in during cascades, start 10 free spins. Here the multiplier ladder is bigger: x2, x4, x6, x10. When a golden symbol is eliminated in free spins, its whole reel turns Wild.

Buy feature

Some versions let you buy the free spins for 50x the bet (75x with Extra Bet). Availability depends on where the game is offered.

Taglish tip: Sa demo, panoorin kung ilang cascade ang kailangan bago umabot sa x5. Makikita mo agad na bihira ito, kaya hindi “sure” ang malaking multiplier kahit matagal ka nang naglalaro.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
Ilang free spins ang makukuha sa Sugar Bang Bang?
Three gummy-bear scatters give 10 free spins. Landing three more during the bonus adds 5 spins.
Ano ang Extra Bet sa Sugar Bang Bang?
Extra Bet raises the stake by 50% and opens three extra positions on the middle reels, taking the game from 1,024 to 2,000 ways.
