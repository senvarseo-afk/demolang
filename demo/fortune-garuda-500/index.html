Quick answer

Fortune Garuda 500 is a 2026 JILI slot on a 3x3 grid with 5 lines and a side reel of multipliers. Its twist is the NUDGE: the side reel can be nudged up to 500 times, and every multiplier that passes the center is added to the total, up to 500x. JILI rates it low-to-medium volatility with a 25,000x max win. Try the Fortune Garuda 500 demo free here.

Paano laruin

3x3 grid with a side reel

Like the Fortune Gems games, the main grid is three reels by three rows with five lines. The extra reel on the side holds the multipliers.

Ex NUDGE

When the Garuda’s power triggers, the side reel nudges down. Every multiplier that crosses the center is added up, and there can be up to 500 nudges and a multiplier of up to 500x.

Gift of Garuda

A random feature that can upgrade multipliers, add more nudges or give a respin.

Taglish tip: Kakaiba ang Fortune Garuda 500 dahil nagdadagdag ang multiplier habang gumagalaw ang side reel. Panoorin sa demo kung paano ito nag-iipon bago mo intindihin ang 25,000x.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
May 'Fortune Garuda' ba na walang 500?

Ano ang NUDGE sa Fortune Garuda 500?
