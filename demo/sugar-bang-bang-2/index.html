Quick answer

Sugar Bang Bang 2 is Fa Chai's April 2025 sequel to its candy cascade slot. It keeps the 5x4 grid and 1,024 ways, the 96.5% RTP and medium volatility, but raises the top win to 5,000x the bet. The new piece is the Featured Wild, which only appears after a golden symbol is cleared and then copies itself across the reels. Play the demo here free, with virtual credits.

Paano laruin

Cascades and a multiplier that resets

Winning symbols disappear and new ones fall in. The multiplier steps up with each cascade (1x, 2x, 3x, up to 5x) and drops back once the cascade chain ends.

Golden symbols, now worth more

Golden symbols land on reels 2 to 4. When one is part of a win it becomes a Wild and adds +1x to the multiplier, something the first game did not do.

Featured Wild

This Wild only shows up after a golden symbol has been eliminated. It then copies itself onto other positions as extra Wilds (never over a scatter), which is how the bigger wins in this sequel usually build.

Free spins

Three scatters give 10 free spins with a multiplier ladder of 2x, 4x, 6x and 10x. Three more scatters in the bonus add 5 spins.

Extra Bet at buy feature

Extra Bet costs 1.5x the stake. Golden tickets can then replace golden symbols (always in free spins), turn Wild and add +2x. The bonus buy, where offered, costs 50x (75x with Extra Bet).

Taglish tip: Kung galing ka sa unang Sugar Bang Bang, subukan muna ang demo nang walang Extra Bet para makita ang pagkakaiba ng Featured Wild.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. RTP meaning, explained.
