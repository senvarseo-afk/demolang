Quick answer

Fortune Gems is a classic-style JILI slot: a 3x3 grid, 5 paylines, and a fourth reel of multipliers from x1 to x15 that applies to every win in the spin. Paano laruin

3x3 grid at 5 lines

Three gem reels, five fixed lines. The Mask symbol is wild and substitutes for the gems; three Masks on a line pay 5x on their own.

The multiplier reel

The fourth reel on the right shows x1, x2, x3, x5, x10 or x15. The value in the center multiplies the whole spin’s win. A small line hit with x15 is how the bigger results happen, which is also why the max win is only 375x.

Taglish tip: Mababa ang max win pero madalas ang maliliit na panalo. Sa demo, subukan ang may EX at walang EX para makita mismo ang pagkakaiba.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
Ano ang EX o Extra Bet sa Fortune Gems?
Extra Bet costs 50% more per spin and makes the higher multipliers on the fourth reel land more often. It does not change the 375x cap.
May free spins ba ang Fortune Gems?
No. There is no scatter and no free spins. The multiplier reel is the only feature.
