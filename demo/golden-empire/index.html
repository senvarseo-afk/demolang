Paano laruin

Ways, cascades and big symbols

Six reels plus a top reel over reels 2 to 5 give up to 32,400 ways. Winning symbols are removed and new ones fall in. JILI notes that larger symbols can be removed up to four times.

Golden symbols at wilds

Golden-framed symbols on reels 2 to 5 turn into wilds after they are part of a win.

Free spins with an uncapped multiplier

Four scatters start the free game (8 spins, +2 for each extra scatter, according to Demoslot). Here the multiplier goes up with every removal and has no cap, and it carries over through the bonus.

Taglish tip: Ang laki ng panalo dito ay galing sa multiplier na tuloy-tuloy na tumataas sa free spins. Sa base game, normal na maliit lang ang panalo.

Volatility, max win, ways and the uncapped free-game multiplier are from JILI’s game page. Scatter counts are from Demoslot only and will be checked in the demo.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
Ilang scatter ang kailangan sa Golden Empire?
Four scatters start 8 free spins, with 2 more for each extra scatter (per Demoslot). We will confirm this against the in-game paytable.
Ano ang 32,400 ways?
The number of reel positions changes from spin to spin. When every reel shows its maximum number of symbols, there are 32,400 ways to make a win.
