Quick answer

Wild Bandito is a 2021 PG Soft slot with a Mexican bandit theme, on a 5x4 grid with 1,024 ways. PG Soft lists a 96.73% RTP, medium volatility and a 25,000x max win. The win multiplier rises by 1 with every cascade, and in free spins every symbol on the middle reel lands gold-framed, so wilds come more often. Play the Wild Bandito demo free here, demo lang.

Paano laruin

1,024 ways with a rising multiplier

Five reels, four rows. Every winning cascade raises the multiplier by 1 (x1, x2, x3 and so on). In the base game it resets each round; in free spins it carries through.

Gold-framed symbols

Gold-framed symbols turn wild after they are part of a win, which keeps cascades going.

Free spins

Three scatters give 12 free spins. In the bonus, every symbol on reel 3 lands gold-framed, and the multiplier keeps climbing for the whole feature.

Taglish tip: Mabagal pero tuloy-tuloy ang pagtaas ng multiplier dito, +1 kada cascade. Sa demo, makikita mo kung bakit mahalaga ang reel 3 sa free spins.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
