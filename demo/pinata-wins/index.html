Quick answer

Pinata Wins is a PG Soft slot with a Mexican fiesta theme, on a 5x3 grid with 20 fixed lines. PG Soft lists a 96.76% RTP, medium volatility and a 5,000x max win. Paano laruin

20 lines with cascades

Pinata Wins is a line game: 5 reels, 3 rows, 20 fixed lines. Winning symbols are cleared and new ones fall in, so one spin can pay more than once. The Aelia wild substitutes for everything except the scatter.

Gold-framed symbols and multipliers

Gold-framed symbols land on reels 2 to 4. They carry multipliers of up to x100 and turn wild after they are part of a win.

Free spins

Three Star Piñata scatters give 15 free spins. Here the multipliers you collect are kept at the top of the reels until the end of the feature instead of disappearing, which is where the 5,000x top win comes from.

Taglish tip: Sa free spins, tingnan ang taas ng reels. Doon naiipon ang multipliers, kaya mas malaki ang epekto ng huling spins kaysa sa una.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
