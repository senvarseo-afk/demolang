Quick answer

Fortune Gems 2 keeps the original's 3x3 grid, 5 lines and multiplier reel, then adds a Lucky Wheel worth 1x to 1,000x. JILI rates it medium volatility and lists a 10,000x max win, which in practice comes from the wheel with Extra Bet. SlotCatalog lists a 97% RTP. If you liked Fortune Gems but wanted a bonus round, this is the one. Try the demo here free.

Paano laruin

Same base game

Three reels of gems on a 3x3 grid, five lines, a wild symbol and a fourth reel with multipliers from x1 to x15.

Lucky Wheel

If a wheel symbol lands in the center of the special reel, you get one spin of the Lucky Wheel, worth 1x to 1,000x the bet. JILI says scatters raise the chance of reaching the wheel.

Extra Bet

Extra Bet costs 50% more. It removes x1 from the multiplier reel and multiplies wheel prizes by x1 to x15. The 10,000x top win is only possible this way.

Taglish tip: Kung galing ka sa Fortune Gems, subukan ang demo nito nang may at walang Extra Bet. Makikita mo agad na mas bihira pero mas malaki ang panalo.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. RTP meaning, explained.
Ano ang pinagkaiba ng Fortune Gems 2 sa Fortune Gems?
Fortune Gems 2 adds the Lucky Wheel (1x to 1,000x) and raises the max win from 375x to 10,000x. With Extra Bet, the x1 is removed from the multiplier reel and wheel prizes can be multiplied.
