Quick answer

Sweet Bonanza is Pragmatic Play's 2019 candy slot and still one of the best-known slots in the world. It pays anywhere on a 6x5 grid for 8 or more matching symbols, tumbles after every win, and in free spins adds multiplier bombs from x2 to x100. Pragmatic lists a 96.48% RTP; volatility is medium-high and the max win is about 21,100x. Play the Sweet Bonanza demo free here.

Paano laruin

Pay anywhere and tumble

Eight or more matching fruits or candies anywhere on the grid pay. They disappear and new symbols tumble down, so one spin can pay several times.

Free spins with multiplier bombs

Four or more scatters give 10 free spins. In the bonus, rainbow bombs with x2 to x100 can land. Their values are added together at the end of the tumble sequence and applied to that spin’s win.

Ante bet and buy

Ante bet adds 25% to the stake for about double the free-spin chance. Where allowed, free spins can be bought for 100x.

The base rules did not change: same grid, same 8-symbol pay-anywhere rule, same 10 free spins. The 1000 version simply moves more of the value into rare, very large bombs, which is why it feels quieter for longer.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
Ilang scatter para sa Sweet Bonanza free spins?
Four to six lollipop scatters give 10 free spins and also pay up to 100x. Three more scatters in the bonus add 5 spins.
Sweet Bonanza o Sweet Bonanza 1000?
The original has smaller bombs (up to x100) and slightly lower volatility. Sweet Bonanza 1000 has bombs up to x1,000 and a 25,000x max win.
