Paano laruin

7x7 cluster pays

Five or more matching candies touching horizontally or vertically make a win. They explode and new candies tumble in until no new clusters form.

Multiplier spots

Every exploded symbol leaves its spot marked. A second win on that spot starts a x2 multiplier, doubling each time up to x1,024. In the base game the spots clear at the end of each round.

Free spins

Three or more scatters give 10 to 30 free spins. The marked spots and their multipliers stay for the whole bonus, which is how the 25,000x top win is reached. Where allowed, free spins can be bought for 100x, and a Super Bonus for 500x starts with multipliers already placed.

What does RTP mean?
RTP (return to player) is the share of all bets a slot is designed to pay back over a very large number of spins. It says nothing about your next spin. Read the guide.
